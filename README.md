# CineVerse 3D - Theater Seat Booking Experience 🎬💺

A web application with **real-time 3D interactive cinema seat booking** built with Three.js, WebGL, HTML5 & CSS3.

## 🌟 Highlights
- **Full 3D Interactive Cinema Hall**: Procedural curved IMAX screen, stepped riser auditorium floor, projector beam lighting, and 3D individual cinema recliner seats.
- **Seat Point of View (POV)**: Switch camera directly to your selected seat at eye-level to check the exact screen viewing angle before booking!
- **Dynamic Camera Modes**:
  - `Cinema View`: Overview perspective of the whole auditorium.
  - `Audience View`: Front-facing view toward the curved IMAX screen.
  - `My Seat POV`: Cinematic smooth transition straight into your chosen chair.
- **Tier & Pricing Management**:
  - Regular Seats (₹300 - ₹380 depending on movie)
  - VIP Recliners (₹550 with premium spacing & recline)
  - Live Total and Seat Badging
- **Ticket Generation**: Modal summary with simulated barcode & confirmation details.

## 🛠️ Built With
- Three.js (WebGL 3D graphics)
- OrbitControls
- Tween.js (Cinematic camera animations)
- Vanilla JavaScript & CSS3 Modern Glassmorphism
